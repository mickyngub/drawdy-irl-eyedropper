# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release

- pick colours from real life through your webcam: tap a spot in the live preview, or put it under the ring and press Pick colour
- a live loupe with the hex code and nearest colour name, read from a small patch that ignores shadows and glints
- Set white with a sheet of paper to correct the camera's colour cast
- the picked colour becomes your pen and shape stroke colour, with a Use for drawing switch to turn that off
- up to 16 swatches that are saved for next time, with near-duplicates folded together
- Paint selection fills shapes, colours text and strokes lines and drawings in one undo step
- Add to board places your swatches as a labelled, grouped palette in view
- clear messages when the camera is blocked, missing, in use or stops, with a button to try again
- the panel follows the board's light and dark themes
